High Yield Test Concepts

  • Left ventricle generates the highest pressure.
  • Pulmonary veins carry oxygenated blood.
  • Pulmonary arteries carry deoxygenated blood.
  • RCA frequently supplies the SA and AV nodes.
  • The SA node is the normal pacemaker of the heart.
  • AV junctional escape rate = 40–60 bpm.
  • Ventricular escape rate = 20–40 bpm.

The Four Chambers of the Heart

Chamber Function
Right Atrium Receives deoxygenated blood from the body
Right Ventricle Pumps blood to the lungs
Left Atrium Receives oxygenated blood from the lungs
Left Ventricle Pumps oxygenated blood to the body
Exam Tip: The left ventricle has the thickest myocardium because it must generate enough pressure to perfuse the entire body.

Self Check

Which vessel returns oxygenated blood to the heart? Pulmonary veins
Which artery carries deoxygenated blood? Pulmonary artery

Heart Valves

Valve Location Function
Tricuspid RA → RV Prevents backflow into the right atrium
Pulmonic RV → Pulmonary Artery Prevents backflow into the right ventricle
Mitral (Bicuspid) LA → LV Prevents backflow into the left atrium
Aortic LV → Aorta Prevents backflow into the left ventricle

Coronary Circulation

The myocardium receives oxygen from the coronary arteries.

Artery Major Supply Area
Left Main Branches into LAD and Circumflex
LAD Anterior wall and septum
Circumflex Lateral wall
RCA Inferior wall, RV, conduction system
Paramedic Pearl: Patients with inferior STEMIs may be bradycardic because RCA occlusions frequently affect SA and AV node perfusion.

Electrophysiology

Automaticity Ability of cardiac cells to spontaneously generate an electrical impulse.
Excitability Ability of cardiac cells to respond to stimulation.
Conductivity Ability to transmit an electrical impulse.
Contractility Ability of myocardial cells to mechanically contract.

NREMT-Style Review Questions

1. Which chamber pumps blood into the systemic circulation?

Answer Left ventricle

2. A patient experiences an LAD occlusion. What area is most likely affected?

Answer Anterior wall and interventricular septum

3. What structure normally initiates the heartbeat?

Answer SA Node

4. Which coronary artery is commonly associated with symptomatic bradycardia during an MI?

Answer Right Coronary Artery

5. Which electrophysiologic property allows the SA node to spontaneously fire?

Answer Automaticity
