Blood Flow Through the Heart

Follow the pathway below until you can recite it from memory.

  1. Body
  2. Superior & Inferior Vena Cava
  3. Right Atrium
  4. Tricuspid Valve
  5. Right Ventricle
  6. Pulmonic Valve
  7. Pulmonary Artery
  8. Lungs
  9. Pulmonary Veins
  10. Left Atrium
  11. Mitral Valve
  12. Left Ventricle
  13. Aortic Valve
  14. Aorta
  15. Body

Knowledge Check

Which vessel carries oxygenated blood back to the heart? Pulmonary Veins
Which artery carries deoxygenated blood? Pulmonary Artery
Chamber Function
Right Atrium Receives deoxygenated blood from the body
Right Ventricle Pumps blood to the lungs
Left Atrium Receives oxygenated blood from the lungs
Left Ventricle Pumps oxygenated blood to systemic circulation

Quick Knowledge Check

Which chamber has the thickest myocardium?

Left Ventricle

Which chamber generates the highest pressure?

Left Ventricle
