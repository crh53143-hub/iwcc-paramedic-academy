Cardiac Anatomy & Physiology

1. Which chamber generates the highest pressure in the heart?

A. Right Atrium
B. Right Ventricle
C. Left Atrium
D. Left Ventricle
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: D. Left Ventricle

The left ventricle must generate enough pressure to perfuse the entire systemic circulation.

2. Which vessel returns oxygenated blood to the left atrium?

A. Pulmonary Artery
B. Aorta
C. Pulmonary Veins
D. Superior Vena Cava
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Pulmonary Veins

3. The intrinsic pacemaker rate of the AV junction is:

A. 20–40 bpm
B. 40–60 bpm
C. 60–100 bpm
D. 100–150 bpm
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. 40–60 bpm

4. Which coronary artery most commonly supplies the anterior wall?

A. RCA
B. Circumflex
C. LAD
D. Coronary Sinus
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. LAD

5. Which property allows the SA node to spontaneously generate impulses?

A. Conductivity
B. Contractility
C. Automaticity
D. Excitability
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Automaticity

ECG Fundamentals

6. The P wave represents:

A. Ventricular depolarization
B. Ventricular repolarization
C. Atrial depolarization
D. Atrial repolarization
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Atrial depolarization

7. The normal PR interval is:

A. 0.04–0.10 sec
B. 0.12–0.20 sec
C. 0.22–0.30 sec
D. 0.30–0.40 sec
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. 0.12–0.20 sec

8. A QRS complex represents:

A. Atrial depolarization
B. Atrial contraction
C. Ventricular depolarization
D. Ventricular repolarization
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Ventricular depolarization

9. Four large boxes between two R waves equals approximately:

A. 50 bpm
B. 60 bpm
C. 75 bpm
D. 100 bpm
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. 75 bpm

10. The T wave represents:

A. Atrial repolarization
B. Ventricular repolarization
C. SA node activity
D. AV node activity
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. Ventricular repolarization

Rhythm Recognition

11. A rhythm has a rate of 84 bpm, is regular, and has a P wave before every QRS. What rhythm is present?

A. Sinus Bradycardia
B. Junctional Rhythm
C. Normal Sinus Rhythm
D. Atrial Tachycardia
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Normal Sinus Rhythm

12. A patient has a heart rate of 42 bpm and a P wave before every QRS. What rhythm is present?

A. Junctional Rhythm
B. Sinus Bradycardia
C. Third Degree Block
D. Idioventricular Rhythm
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. Sinus Bradycardia

13. Which rhythm is described as irregularly irregular?

A. Atrial Flutter
B. Atrial Fibrillation
C. Sinus Arrhythmia
D. Mobitz I
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. Atrial Fibrillation

14. Sawtooth flutter waves are characteristic of:

A. Atrial Tachycardia
B. MAT
C. Atrial Flutter
D. Junctional Tachycardia
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Atrial Flutter

15. Three different P-wave morphologies with a heart rate of 130 bpm suggests:

A. WAP
B. MAT
C. PACs
D. Atrial Flutter
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. MAT

AV Blocks & Ventricular Rhythms

16. Which AV block demonstrates progressively lengthening PR intervals followed by a dropped QRS?

A. First Degree
B. Mobitz I
C. Mobitz II
D. Third Degree
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. Mobitz I

17. Which AV block has the highest risk of progressing to complete heart block?

A. First Degree
B. Sinus Bradycardia
C. Mobitz I
D. Mobitz II
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: D. Mobitz II

18. Which rhythm should be assumed when a patient presents with a wide-complex tachycardia?

A. SVT
B. Junctional Tachycardia
C. Ventricular Tachycardia
D. Atrial Flutter
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Ventricular Tachycardia

19. Which rhythm is associated with a prolonged QT interval and twisting QRS complexes?

A. VF
B. Atrial Fibrillation
C. Torsades de Pointes
D. AIVR
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: C. Torsades de Pointes

20. Which rhythms are shockable?

A. PEA and Asystole
B. VF and Pulseless VT
C. Asystole and VT with pulse
D. PEA and VF
Answer & Rationale Correct Answer: B. VF and Pulseless VT
