Why Do We Use the ECG?

The electrocardiogram (ECG) records the electrical activity of the heart. Remember that the ECG does not directly show mechanical contractions. It records the electrical events that trigger myocardial contraction.

Test Tip: Electrical activity does not always equal a pulse. Always treat the patient, not just the monitor.

Memorize These Numbers

  • Small Box = 0.04 sec
  • Large Box = 0.20 sec
  • 1 Minute = 300 Large Boxes

Components of the ECG

Component Meaning
P Wave Atrial Depolarization
PR Interval Atrial-to-Ventricular Conduction Time
QRS Complex Ventricular Depolarization
ST Segment Early Ventricular Repolarization
T Wave Ventricular Repolarization

Normal ECG Values

Measurement Normal Value
PR Interval 0.12 - 0.20 sec
QRS Duration ≤ 0.12 sec
Adult Sinus Rate 60 - 100 bpm

How to Calculate Heart Rate

300 Method

Large Boxes Rate
1 300
2 150
3 100
4 75
5 60
6 50

Quick Rule

300 ÷ Number of Large Boxes = Heart Rate

4 Large Boxes Between R Waves = What Rate? 75 bpm
2 Large Boxes Between R Waves = What Rate? 150 bpm

Rhythm Analysis Process

  1. Determine the Heart Rate
  2. Determine Rhythm Regularity
  3. Evaluate P Waves
  4. Measure the PR Interval
  5. Measure the QRS Duration
  6. Determine the Rhythm Origin
  7. Assess Patient Stability
Always use the same rhythm interpretation method every time.

What Questions Should You Ask?

  • Is the rhythm regular?
  • Is there a P wave before every QRS?
  • Does every P wave produce a QRS?
  • Is the PR interval normal?
  • Is the QRS narrow or wide?
  • What is the heart rate?
  • Is the patient stable or unstable?

Clinical Application

A patient has:

  • Rate = 42 bpm
  • Regular rhythm
  • P wave before every QRS
  • PR = 0.16 sec
  • QRS = 0.08 sec

Questions

Is the rhythm sinus in origin? Yes. Every P wave is followed by a QRS.
What rhythm is most likely present? Sinus Bradycardia

NREMT Challenge Questions

1. A rhythm demonstrates a rate of 82 bpm, regular R-R intervals, and a P wave before every QRS. What rhythm is present?

Answer Normal Sinus Rhythm

2. What ECG component represents ventricular depolarization?

Answer QRS Complex

3. Which wave represents ventricular repolarization?

Answer T Wave

4. Which interval evaluates conduction from the atria to the ventricles?

Answer PR Interval

5. Why should patient assessment always accompany rhythm interpretation?

Answer Electrical activity does not always produce effective mechanical activity or adequate perfusion.
